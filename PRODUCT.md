# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- Primary: Busy professionals (30–50 years old) needing fast news summaries.
- Secondary: News junkies, students, and content creators repurposing news.
- Context: "On-the-go" consumption; users need high-signal, low-noise information during brief windows of attention.

## Product Purpose
Newsreel AI transforms abstract news topics into cinematic, source-cited video summaries. It bridges the trust gap in AI by providing explicit source attribution, turning passive reading into memorable visual context in under 90 seconds.

## Brand Personality
- Trustworthy
- Minimalist
- On-the-go

## Anti-references
- "AI slop" clichés (generic bot icons, wavy spark gradients).
- Emojis in UI or copy.
- Neon-glow overkill and "dark mode glow up" aesthetics.
- Gradients and neon color palettes.
- Nested cards ("cards on cards").
- Status dots.

## Design Principles
- **Surgical Precision**: Strip away every element that doesn't serve the "on-the-go" professional. Minimalism is not just an aesthetic, but a speed requirement.
- **Radical Transparency**: The user must never guess what the system is doing. Loading states should be granular and descriptive, exposing the research and generation pipeline.
- **Source First**: Information integrity is the product. Sources are not "extra" data; they are the foundation of the visual hierarchy.
- **Anti-Glimmer**: Avoid the "magical AI" aesthetic. Use steady, functional motion and solid, authoritative colors to convey stability and reliability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Prioritize high-legibility typography for "on-the-go" reading.
- Ensure loading progress is screen-reader accessible and visually distinct without relying on overstimulating motion.
- Focus on transparent system feedback to reduce cognitive load during the generation wait time.
